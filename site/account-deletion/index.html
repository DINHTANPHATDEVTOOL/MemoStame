MemoStamp Account Deletion

Official Data Deletion & Privacy Rights Guide • MemoStamp (by Mipa Studio)

Overview

At MemoStamp, we believe you should always be in full control of your personal data. You can delete your account and all associated personal content at any time, either directly within the mobile application or by submitting an external request.

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant & Recommended)

If you have access to the MemoStamp mobile app, this is the quickest and most direct method:

  1. Open the MemoStamp app on your Android or iOS device.
  2. Navigate to your Passport / Profile screen.
  3. Tap the Settings button (gear icon).
  4. Select Xóa tài khoản vĩnh viễn / Delete Account Permanently.
  5. Enter your account password to confirm ownership.
  6. Tap Xác nhận xóa / Confirm Deletion.

Upon confirmation, your deletion request is processed immediately by our server.

What Data Is Deleted vs Retained

Data Category Action Taken
Authentication credentials (Email, Password hash) Permanently deleted immediately from Supabase Auth.
User Profile (Display name, bio, avatar) Permanently deleted via database cascading delete.
Stamp Photo Media Permanently purged from encrypted cloud storage bucket.
Stamps, Postmark stories & Geolocation Permanently deleted from database.
Feed Posts, Comments, Replies & Reactions Permanently deleted.
Direct Messages & Chat History Permanently deleted.
Push Device Tokens (FCM & APNs) Permanently purged; notification delivery stops immediately.
Friendships & User Blocks Permanently dissolved.
Accepted Stamp Trades (Recipient Copies) Anonymized: If you traded a stamp copy to another user, their legitimate copy remains in their album. However, your account linkage is permanently set to NULL (anonymized), removing any trace of your profile.

Method 2: External Deletion Request (Web / Email)

If you have uninstalled the application or can no longer log in, you can request account deletion by emailing our support team:

Send an email to the support address listed on the official MemoStamp Google Play or Apple App Store listing with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Please include:

  • The registered email address associated with your MemoStamp account.
Security Notice:

We will NEVER ask you for your account password, security tokens, or credit card information. To protect your privacy and prevent unauthorized deletion, we will send a verification confirmation link to your registered email before processing the request.

External manual deletion requests are processed within 30 days of identity verification.
